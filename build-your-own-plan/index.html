How it works

  1. Pick a starting point, or begin empty. Then drag dogs from All dogs into Coming to the New Haven.
  2. Drag them from there into a room or yard. You can move them again any time.
  3. Happy? Give your plan a name and publish it for others to see.

On a phone, press and hold a dog, then drag. Or just tap a dog and then tap Put here on the place you want.
